Recipe of the Month

Krum Kake

  • 3 eggs, well beaten
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • Pinch of cardamom

Put about 1 teaspoon of batter in the krum kake iron and bake until brown on both sides. Roll quickly around a stick to form a cone shape.
